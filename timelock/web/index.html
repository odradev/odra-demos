What a timelock is for

Every change is announced before it happens

A timelock holds the admin keys of other contracts. A change it makes, a new fee, an upgrade or a new admin, is first proposed in public and can be executed only after a delay. Users who do not like what is coming have time to leave before it does.

  • Proposals are public: anyone can read what will change and when.
  • Nobody can skip the wait, not even whoever proposed the change.
  • A proposal can be cancelled until it is executed.
