What an auction is

Sell an NFT to whoever bids the most

You put an NFT up with a lowest price and an end time. Bidders outbid each other in CSPR, each bid at least 5% above the last. When the time is up, the highest bidder gets the NFT and you get their CSPR.

  • The NFT and every bid sit in a smart contract until the auction is settled.
  • Outbid? Take your CSPR back whenever you like, even before the end.
  • A bid in the last 5 minutes moves the end 5 minutes later, so nobody wins by sniping.
  • Anyone can settle an auction that has ended. With no bids, the NFT goes back to the seller.
