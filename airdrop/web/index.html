What an airdrop is

Give tokens to a long list of people, and let each of them claim their own

You put the tokens for the whole list into the contract in one transaction. The list itself stays off the chain: the contract keeps only its fingerprint, a Merkle root, 32 bytes worked out from every line, so that changing any line changes it. Each recipient shows their line and the few pieces that connect it to the fingerprint, and receives their amount.

  • One transaction for you, whether the list has three names or thousands.
  • Each recipient pays the fee of their own claim, about 1.5 CSPR, and can claim only once.
  • Set a deadline, and after it you can take back what nobody claimed.
