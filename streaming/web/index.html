What payment streaming is

Pay someone by the second, not once a month

A stream pays a recipient continuously. You deposit the whole amount up front, and it becomes theirs a little every second, from the start time to the end time. Salaries, grants and subscriptions, with no invoices and no waiting for payday.

  • The deposit sits in a smart contract, out of reach of both sides.
  • The recipient withdraws what has reached them whenever they like, all of it or a part.
  • You can stop a stream at any time: they get what has reached them, you get the rest back.
