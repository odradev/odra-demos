What a payment splitter is

Share every payment by fixed parts

List who gets what share, once. From then on anyone can pay tokens into the split, and each payee withdraws their part whenever they like. Nobody, the creator included, can change the shares or take someone else's part.

  • For a team, a band or a joint project that is paid together and shares the income.
  • Any CEP-18 token. Payments are counted per split and per token.
  • One contract holds every split. It has no owner and takes no fees.
