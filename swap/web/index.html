What an OTC swap is

Trade two tokens with one person, both sides at once

Over the counter: you agree a price with someone, put your side in a smart contract and send them a link. When they take the offer, both sides change hands in the same transaction, or nothing moves. No pool, no price that slips, and nobody has to pay first on trust.

  • You say what you give, what you want for it, and until when the offer stands.
  • Name the one account that may take it, or let anyone with the link take it.
  • Until it is taken you can cancel it and get your tokens back, after it expires too.
