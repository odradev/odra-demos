What a token sale is

Sell your token for CSPR at one fixed price, to everyone or only to a list

You put the tokens on sale in one transaction and set the terms: the price of one token, how many there are, how many one wallet may buy, and when buying opens and closes. A buyer sends CSPR and receives the tokens in the same transaction, from the contract, not from you. For a list of buyers the contract keeps only its fingerprint, a Merkle root, as the airdrop does.

  • The first buyer and the last pay the same price, and nobody can buy past the cap.
  • Take out the CSPR whenever you like, also while the sale runs.
  • After the sale closes, take back the tokens nobody bought.
