What crowdfunding with a goal is

Raise tokens for an idea, all or nothing

Set a goal and a deadline, and let anyone back your idea with tokens. If the pledges reach the goal in time, you get all of them. If they fall short, every backer takes their pledge back, so nobody pays for an idea that did not get enough support.

  • Pledges sit in a contract with no admin and no fees until the deadline.
  • A pledge cannot be taken back while the campaign runs, so the total only grows.
  • Every campaign is public: anyone can see how far it has got, no wallet needed.
