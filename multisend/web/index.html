What multisend is

Pay many accounts with one signature

Paste a list of recipients and amounts, check it, sign once. Everyone on the list is paid in the same transaction: all of them or, if anything is wrong, none.

  • Works for CSPR and for CEP-18 tokens.
  • Tokens go straight from your wallet to each recipient; the contract holds nothing.
  • It saves signing, not fees: the network charges about 1 CSPR per recipient for a token and about 2 CSPR per recipient for CSPR.
