What a liquidity lock is

Show everyone that your liquidity cannot be pulled

When a project adds its token to a DEX, it gets pool tokens that can take the liquidity back out at any moment. Locking them here until a date proves, on chain, that it cannot do so before that date.

  • The tokens sit in a contract with no admin and no fees. Nobody can take them early.
  • The unlock date can only move later, never earlier.
  • Anyone can paste a token address below and see what is locked, by whom and until when.
