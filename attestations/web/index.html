What an attestation is

Vouch for an account on chain, and let any app or contract check it

An issuer records a short statement about an account, such as "passed KYC" or "took part in the hackathon". Anyone can read it, and another contract can ask the registry whether it holds before it lets the account in. The issuer can give it an end date, and take it back if the kind of statement allows that.

  • A kind of statement is a schema. Whoever registers one is its only issuer.
  • A statement is valid until it expires or is revoked. Attesting again replaces it.
  • No tokens: a statement costs only the fee of one transaction, about 2 CSPR.
