What vesting is

Pay tokens out gradually, not all at once

Vesting locks tokens for a teammate, an investor or a contributor and unlocks them a little at a time. They wait through an opening period, the cliff, and after it their tokens unlock steadily until the end date.

  • The locked tokens sit in a smart contract, out of reach of both sides.
  • The recipient claims what has unlocked whenever they like.
  • If you choose so, you can stop a schedule and take back what has not unlocked yet.
