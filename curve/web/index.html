What a bonding curve is

A token that sells itself, at a price that rises with every sale

Pay CSPR and the contract mints CURVE tokens to you. Every token it sells makes the next one a little dearer, along a straight line. Sell your tokens back whenever you like: the contract burns them and pays you from the CSPR that buyers paid in, at the price on the line.

  • The shaded area under the line is the reserve: enough CSPR to buy back every token.
  • No owner and no fees. The contract rounds every price a fraction of a mote in its own favour.
  • Everything on this page is read from the contract, no wallet needed.

How the price is set

The same arithmetic runs in the contract and on this page.

A token costs — CSPR when none exists, and — CSPR more for every token in existence. What a purchase costs is the area under the line between the supply before and after it, so a large purchase pays the rising price for every part of itself. A sale pays the area it takes away.

The formula

price(supply) = base + slope × supply
cost(a) = base × a + slope × (2 × supply × a + a²) / 2

Purchases round up and sales round down, to the mote. Buying and selling back the same amount therefore never returns more than was paid in, and the reserve always covers every holder. Each transaction carries a minimum 1% below the quote: if someone else trades first and moves the price further, it fails instead of filling at a worse price.
