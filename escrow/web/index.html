What an escrow is

Pay a stranger only once the deal is done

The buyer's tokens wait in a smart contract, out of reach of both sides, until the deal is settled. A freelance job, a second-hand sale, an over-the-counter trade: neither side has to pay or deliver first on trust.

  • The buyer releases the payment to the seller when satisfied; the seller can send it back.
  • If they disagree, either side opens a dispute, and the arbiter both agreed on decides who gets the tokens, or splits them.
  • If nobody opens a dispute by the deadline, the buyer can take the tokens back.
