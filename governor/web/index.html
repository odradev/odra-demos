What a governor is for

Token holders decide, a timelock carries it out

A governor lets the holders of a token decide how a service is run; here, its fee. Anyone can propose a new fee, holders vote for or against it with their tokens, and a fee that wins goes to a timelock, which sets it after a public delay.

  • You vote by depositing tokens, and take them back when the vote ends.
  • A fee passes with enough tokens in favour and more for than against.
  • Nobody, not even the winners, can skip the timelock's delay.
